Placement reliability

0–4
0 — Survey-grade
Precise coordinates from direct survey and/or geodetic control.

1 — Georeferenced sources
Indirect placement from georeferenced plans/datasets, reliable external GIS sources and/or extant features.

2 — Remote/cartographic approximation
Indirect placement from cartography and/or satellite imagery enabling a good positional approximation.

3 — Qualitative approximation
Indirect placement from qualitative evidence (oral/bibliographic); treat location and size as approximate.

4 — Symbolic location
Symbolic/representative placement (not a measured position).
